Nikolas Sung

Post-Baccalaureate Research Scholar

I'm a K. Lisa Yang Post-Baccalaureate Research Scholar in the Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences at MIT, where I work in the EvLab with Dr. Evelina Fedorenko.

My research asks how language experience shapes the brain, and how existing neural circuitry and cognitive architecture alters our language use. I've used EEG, eye-tracking, and computational modeling to explore how bilingual individuals organize meaning and memory across two languages.

Before my Post-Bac, I graduated from Northwestern University in 2026 with a B.A. in Neuroscience and Linguistics and a minor in Business Institutions. There, I worked with Dr. Viorica Marian in the Bilingualism and Psycholinguistics Research Lab, and I spent time at the University of Granada in Dr. Teresa Bajo's Memory and Language Group.
